Rubik's Cube solver robot

General

After finishing my bachelor's thesis, I felt that the project wasn’t engaging enough for someone without programming experience. Therefore, I decided to develop some hardware around the software concept and modify certain aspects of it.

After considering several different methods, I decided to go with the below shown one (the one with the claws). As the project is still in its early stages, detailed plans are still needed before building an actual prototype.

Technical details:

In order to have a working robot I need the following things:

  • An Arduino
  • A Raspberry Pi
  • 4 pieces of 180 servo motors for the rotating grips
  • 4 pieces of 360 servo motors for the release mechanism
  • 1.5x15mm steel bar for the frame of the robot
